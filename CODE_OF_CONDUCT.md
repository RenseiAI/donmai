# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone participating in Donmai's issues, pull requests, discussions, and
community spaces deserves a welcoming environment, regardless of background,
identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give constructive feedback and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect people's privacy, time, and stated boundaries.
- Make room for newcomers and different experiences.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, and publishing another person's private information without permission
are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Include links or other relevant context, and avoid posting private details in
public threads. Maintainers will handle reports with discretion and share
information only as needed to investigate and respond.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, request a change in behavior,
restrict participation, or ban participants depending on the severity and
pattern of behavior. These expectations apply to maintainers as well as other
contributors and to people representing Donmai in public spaces.
